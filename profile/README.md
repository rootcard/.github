# RootCard Open

**An open, portable identity for online logins — an ID card you own.**

RootCard is an open-source identity solution designed to put people in control of their online identity. Your identity is backed by a recovery key you keep, not by a central identity provider. A password unlocks your encrypted account on your device; it is never sent to the server. You can use RootCard to add password-based sign-in to your own service, or prove your identity to other services without relying on a central sign-in provider.

## Project status

RootCard is pre-1.0. Its identity format is a draft and has not yet had an external security review. Please review the [project status and security notes](https://github.com/rootcard/rootcard#rootcard) before relying on it for high-value accounts.
